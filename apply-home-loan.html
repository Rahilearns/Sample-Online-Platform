Purpose

A Home Loan can be used for the following purposes:

  • Purchase of a new apartment or independent house
  • Construction on owned residential land
  • Renovation, extension or interior finishing of an existing home
  • Take-over (refinancing) of an existing home loan from another bank or NBFI
  • Purchase of residential land for self-construction (with construction plan)

Eligibility

  • Bangladeshi national
  • Age: 21–65 years (at loan maturity)
  • Minimum monthly income: BDT 40,000 (salaried) or equivalent verifiable business income
  • Minimum 2 years of employment or business continuity
  • Acceptable credit history per Credit Information Bureau (CIB) report

Policy Highlights

  • Maximum loan amount: up to BDT 5 crore, capped at 70% of property market value
  • Tenor: 1–25 years
  • Interest rate: 9.50% – 12.00% per annum (floating, subject to product policy)
  • Processing fee: 1.0% of approved loan amount (one-time)
  • Partial / full prepayment allowed after 12 months with 0.5% fee on the prepaid balance
  • Property mortgage and a personal guarantor are required

Required Documents

  • NID copy (both sides) of applicant and co-applicant
  • Latest 6 months' bank statement (primary account)
  • Salary certificate, pay slip and form-V (for salaried applicants)
  • Trade license and 12 months' business account statement (for business owners)
  • e-TIN certificate and most recent tax return acknowledgment
  • 2 passport-size photos
  • Property documents: title deed, mutation, rent receipt, RAJUK/CDA approval
  • Approved building plan (for construction or renovation)

Terms & Conditions

  1. Loan disbursement is subject to credit approval, verification and successful property valuation.
  2. Interest rates are floating and may be revised in line with Bangladesh Bank guidelines.
  3. Late payment incurs a penalty as per the policy in effect at the time of default.
  4. Property mortgage in favour of IDLC Finance PLC is required before disbursement.
  5. Applicant must maintain a primary banking relationship for EMI auto-debit.
  6. All disputes are subject to the jurisdiction of the courts of Bangladesh.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the maximum Home Loan amount I can get?

Up to BDT 5 crore, subject to your repayment capacity and capped at 70% of the property's market value (the lower of the two applies).

How long does approval take?

Typically 7–14 business days after submission of complete documentation and property valuation.

Can I prepay my loan early?

Yes. Partial or full prepayment is allowed after 12 months from disbursement, at a fee of 0.5% on the prepaid balance.

Is a guarantor required?

One personal guarantor is generally required, depending on the loan amount and applicant profile.

Are joint applications allowed?

Yes — spouse, parents or siblings may apply jointly. Combined income improves eligibility.
