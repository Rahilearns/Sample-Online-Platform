Purpose

A Personal Loan can be used for any legitimate personal expense, including:

  • Medical expenses and emergency healthcare
  • Higher-education tuition and study costs
  • Wedding and family ceremony expenses
  • Travel and tourism
  • Home appliances, furniture and consumer-durable purchases
  • Consolidation of existing high-cost debts

Eligibility

  • Bangladeshi national
  • Age: 22–60 years (at loan maturity)
  • Minimum monthly income: BDT 30,000 (salaried) or equivalent verifiable business income
  • Minimum 1 year of employment or 2 years of business continuity
  • Acceptable credit history per Credit Information Bureau (CIB) report

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to pledge any collateral?

No. A Personal Loan is unsecured. A personal guarantor may be required above a defined ticket size.

How long does approval take?

Typically 3–5 business days after submission of complete documentation.

Can I get a top-up after some EMIs are paid?

Yes — top-up is generally available after 12 successful EMIs, subject to fresh credit assessment.

What is the minimum income requirement?

BDT 30,000 per month for salaried applicants. Business owners need verifiable income equivalent.

Will my CIB / credit history be checked?

Yes — a CIB check is mandatory. Adverse history may affect approval or pricing.
