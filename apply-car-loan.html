Apply for a Car Loan

Purpose

A Car Loan can be used for:

  • Purchase of a brand-new private car or SUV from an authorised dealer
  • Purchase of a reconditioned or used vehicle (up to a defined age limit)
  • Take-over (refinancing) of an existing auto loan from another lender
  • Purchase of a small commercial vehicle for personal-business use

Eligibility

  • Bangladeshi national
  • Age: 21–65 years (at loan maturity)
  • Minimum monthly income: BDT 35,000 (salaried) or equivalent verifiable business income
  • Minimum 1 year of employment or 2 years of business continuity
  • Valid driving licence preferred

Policy Highlights

  • Maximum loan amount: up to BDT 1 crore, capped at 80% of vehicle value (new) or 60% (used)
  • Tenor: 1–7 years (subject to vehicle age)
  • Interest rate: 10.00% – 14.00% per annum (floating, subject to product policy)
  • Processing fee: 1.0% of approved loan amount (one-time)
  • Vehicle to be registered in applicant's name and hypothecated to IDLC
  • Comprehensive insurance required throughout the loan tenor

Required Documents

  • NID copy (both sides)
  • Latest 6 months' bank statement
  • Salary certificate, pay slip and form-V (for salaried applicants)
  • Trade license and 12 months' business account statement (for business owners)
  • e-TIN certificate and latest tax return acknowledgment
  • 2 passport-size photos
  • Vehicle quotation / pro-forma invoice from dealer
  • Driving licence copy (where applicable)

Terms & Conditions

  1. Loan disbursement is subject to credit approval and vehicle verification.
  2. Interest rates are floating and may be revised in line with Bangladesh Bank guidelines.
  3. Late payment incurs a penalty as per the policy in effect.
  4. Vehicle is hypothecated to IDLC Finance PLC until full settlement.
  5. Comprehensive vehicle insurance must be maintained throughout the tenor.
  6. All disputes are subject to the jurisdiction of the courts of Bangladesh.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get a loan for a used car?

Yes. Used / reconditioned vehicles are eligible up to a maximum age limit (typically 5 years for reconditioned cars at the end of tenor).

How much down-payment do I need?

Minimum 20% down-payment for a brand-new car and 40% for a used / reconditioned car.

How long does approval take?

Typically 3–7 business days after submission of complete documentation.

Is insurance mandatory?

Yes. Comprehensive insurance is required for the entire loan tenor, renewed annually.

Can I sell the car before the loan is fully repaid?

Only after full settlement of the loan and release of the hypothecation in favour of IDLC.
