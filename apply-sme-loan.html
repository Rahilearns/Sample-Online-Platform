SME Loan |

Purpose

An SME Loan can be used for:

  • Working capital for inventory, raw materials, and operating expenses
  • Purchase of machinery, equipment, or vehicles for the business
  • Expansion of business premises (lease, renovation, fit-out)
  • Long-term project financing and capacity building
  • Women-led enterprise financing under the Purnota programme

Eligibility

  • Bangladesh-registered enterprise with valid trade licence
  • Minimum 2 years of business operation (1 year for Purnota)
  • Proprietor / partner / director aged 21–65 years
  • Acceptable banking and credit history per CIB report
  • Audited or banker-verified financial statements for the latest 1–2 years

Policy Highlights

  • Loan amount: BDT 5 lakh – 5 crore (varies by product and segment)
  • Tenor: 1–5 years (working capital), up to 7 years (long-term)
  • Interest rate: 9.00% – 14.00% per annum (floating, subject to product policy)
  • Processing fee: 1.0% of approved loan amount (one-time)
  • Collateral may be required above defined ticket sizes
  • Concessional rates available under the Purnota women-entrepreneur programme

Required Documents

  • Trade licence (latest, renewed)
  • TIN certificate and most recent tax-return acknowledgment
  • 12 months' business bank account statement
  • NID copies of proprietor / partners / directors
  • Audited or banker-verified financial statements (latest 1–2 years)
  • Memorandum & Articles of Association (for companies)
  • Partnership deed (for partnership firms)
  • Photographs of business premises and the applicant(s)

Terms & Conditions

  1. Loan disbursement is subject to credit approval and verification.
  2. Interest rates are floating and may be revised in line with Bangladesh Bank guidelines.
  3. Late payment incurs a penalty as per the policy in effect.
  4. Applicant must maintain a primary banking relationship for EMI auto-debit.
  5. Periodic monitoring visits to the business premises may be conducted.
  6. All disputes are subject to the jurisdiction of the courts of Bangladesh.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the maximum SME loan amount?

Up to BDT 5 crore depending on the segment, repayment capacity, and security. Smaller ticket sizes are available without collateral.

Is collateral mandatory for an SME loan?

Not for smaller ticket sizes. Larger loans typically require collateral such as property, machinery or third-party guarantee, in line with IDLC's credit policy.

What is the Purnota programme?

Purnota is IDLC's loan programme dedicated to women entrepreneurs, with concessional rates, lighter documentation, and capacity-building support.

How long does approval take?

Typically 7–21 business days after submission of complete documentation, depending on loan size and complexity.

Can I prepay or close the loan early?

Yes. Prepayment is allowed subject to a small fee as per the policy in effect.
